Architectural Factory Workflow.

Mirroring the edge architecture standards of 21dev.handsandhead.com, our Dhaka facility runs an unbroken 5-stage manufacturing sequence with zero subcontracting.

STAGE 01

Raw Sourcing & Tannery Selection

Hides selected directly at certified regional tanneries in Savar. Strict density inspection, chrome-free & vegetable tanning protocols, and tight fiber grading to prevent surface flaking.

Full Grain Veg-Tan Reach Compliant
STAGE 02

Pattern Master & Prototyping

Digital 2D/3D CAD grading from your tech pack or reference garment. First physical sample cut and sewn within 7–10 business days for complete buyer sign-off before line setup.

CAD Grading 3D Simulation 7-Day Turn
STAGE 03

Precision Benchwork

Panel-by-panel edge skiving, hand-burnished seam edges, and bonded #40/#60 nylon thread stitching. Structural horsehair canvas interfacings ensure lapels maintain crisp shape over decades.

Edge Skiving Bonded Nylon Horsehair Canvas

Industrial Hardware Curation

Direct integration of genuine YKK Excella zippers, solid zinc-alloy snap buttons, and 316L stainless steel clasps tested for 15,000+ pull cycles without tarnish or binding.

YKK Excella 316L Steel Anti-Tarnish

Triple-Point Quality & Freight

Every jacket undergoes measurement tolerance checks (±1.5mm), stitch density verification (8 SPI), and metal-detection. Packed in breathable garment bags and routed DDP/FOB worldwide.
